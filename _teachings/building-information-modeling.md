## The Role

I served as a Teaching Assistant for the Building Information Modeling (BIM) course at the University of Tehran. The course material and overall structure were designed by Dr. S. Vahid Faghihi, while Fatemeh Farani and I served as mentors and troubleshooters for students throughout the semester. My primary focus was guiding students through their practical modules and project milestones.

## Course Overview

This term, the course covered an extensive, hands-on workflow including:

- **3D modeling and structural coordination** in Autodesk Revit
- **4D simulation, construction planning, and animations** in Autodesk Navisworks
- **5D cost estimating and Quantity Takeoff (QTO)** utilizing both Revit and Navisworks
- **Project scheduling and time management** using Microsoft Project

## Format

Due to campus closures and regional challenges, the entire course was conducted virtually this semester. Students watched pre-recorded lecture videos on the university platform, while I held live online mentorship sessions — talking through students' questions and troubleshooting issues with them directly.
