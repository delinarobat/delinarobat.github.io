I took Kaggle's **Introduction to Machine Learning** course as my first step into machine learning and data-driven methods.

The course introduced me to the basic workflow of building machine learning models, from understanding and preparing data to training and evaluating models.

One of the most useful aspects for me was learning to think about a problem in terms of **data, features, models, and predictions**. It gave me a foundation for understanding how machine learning can be applied beyond purely computational problems, including engineering and the built environment.

As a civil engineering graduate interested in BIM, automation, and sustainable development, I am particularly interested in how machine learning can eventually be used to support better decisions in construction and urban systems.

### What I learned

- Fundamentals of machine learning
- Preparing and exploring data
- Decision trees and random forests
- Model evaluation
- Overfitting and underfitting
- Basic machine-learning workflows

### Why I took it

I wanted to develop a foundation in machine learning and understand the principles behind data-driven modeling before exploring more advanced applications in engineering and the built environment.
