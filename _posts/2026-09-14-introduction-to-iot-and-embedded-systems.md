I took **Introduction to the Internet of Things and Embedded Systems** through Coursera to understand how digital systems interact with the physical world.

The course introduced the fundamentals of the Internet of Things (IoT) and embedded systems, including sensors and actuators, hardware and software components, microcontrollers, and networking.

What interested me most was the connection between the **physical and digital worlds**.

An IoT system can sense its environment, collect information, communicate data, and use that information to interact with the physical world. Understanding this relationship is important for thinking about how physical environments can become increasingly connected and data-driven.

This connects closely with my interest in **Digital Twins and smart built environments**. Buildings, infrastructure, and cities can generate large amounts of data through sensors and connected systems. Understanding the technologies behind these systems is therefore an important part of understanding how digital representations of the built environment can evolve.

### What I learned

- Internet of Things
- Embedded systems
- Sensors and actuators
- Hardware–software interaction
- Microcontrollers
- Networking fundamentals
- Internet protocols

### Why I took it

I wanted to develop a basic understanding of the technologies that connect physical environments with digital systems, particularly as a foundation for exploring IoT, Digital Twins, and smart built environments.
