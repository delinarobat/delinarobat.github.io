## VR Lab Research Assistant

**Computer Graphics Research Lab, Department of Civil Engineering, University of Tehran**  
**Under the supervision of Dr. Navid Khademi · Summer 2024**

During my internship at the Computer Graphics Research Lab in Civil Engineering at the University of Tehran, I explored the use of **Virtual Reality (VR)** and interactive 3D environments for human-based experiments. The lab focused on developing virtual environments in Unity that could be used to simulate controlled conditions and study users' responses and behavior. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

My work combined **spatial design, computer graphics, and programming**, with a focus on learning Unity and C# and applying them to interactive environments.

## Learning Unity & C#

I began by developing a foundation in **Unity and C#**, learning how to create and manage 3D scenes, work with assets, lighting, cameras, textures, and physical interactions. I also used components such as **Rigidbody** and **Collider** to create more natural interactions between objects. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

As part of this process, I developed small interactive projects to apply programming concepts in practice. One of these exercises was a **Battleship game**, which helped me understand object management, interaction logic, and C# scripting within Unity. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Designing Digital Environments

A major part of my work involved **3D environment design in Unity**. I learned how to combine models, textures, lighting, and environmental assets to construct realistic and interactive spaces.

One of the challenges was adapting available digital assets to local environments. Since many ready-made assets were designed for generic or Western contexts, creating environments that reflected Iranian architectural and cultural characteristics required additional customization and adaptation. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Reconstructing the University of Tehran Campus

I also worked on the digital reconstruction of part of the **central campus of the University of Tehran**.

The project required attention to the dimensions and details of buildings, streets, and open spaces, while also considering the architectural and cultural characteristics of the local context. Generic Unity assets were adapted and customized to create an environment that felt more representative of an Iranian university campus. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

## From Spatial Design to VR Research

Working in the lab introduced me to a different way of thinking about digital environments. Rather than treating 3D models only as visual representations, I learned how virtual environments can become **interactive and controlled spaces for research and human experimentation**. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

The experience strengthened my skills in **Unity, C# programming, 3D environment design, and spatial simulation**, while allowing me to explore the connection between **civil engineering, digital environments, and immersive technologies**.
