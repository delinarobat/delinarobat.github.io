**University of Tehran · Civil Engineering Scientific Student Association**  
**Board Member · September 2024 – January 2026**

As a board member of the Civil Engineering Scientific Student Association at the University of Tehran, I worked with a student team to organize and develop a wide range of **academic, industrial, educational, and student-oriented activities**.

Our work extended beyond individual events. We were involved in the planning, coordination, design, communication, and execution of different programs, while board members regularly moved between different responsibilities depending on the needs of each project.

## Academic & Industry Events

One of the association's main activities was creating opportunities for students to connect **academic education with professional and industrial practice**.

We organized **three events as part of the University of Tehran Industry and Civil Engineering Forum**, bringing together students, academics, and industry professionals.

We also organized events in collaboration with the **Specialized Center of the Steel Industry at the University of Tehran**, providing students with opportunities to learn more about industrial practice and the steel sector.

In addition, we organized conferences and academic sessions introducing different civil engineering specializations, including **Construction Management, Geotechnical Engineering, and other fields**. These programs aimed to help students better understand both the academic pathways and professional applications associated with each specialization.

## Courses & Technical Workshops

The association also organized a variety of **technical courses and educational workshops** for civil engineering students.

One course in which I was directly involved was an **Unreal Engine course**, which introduced students to real-time 3D environments and interactive digital technologies.

My involvement in these programs included working across the organizational process and coordinating with the team responsible for delivering the course to students.


## Site Visits & Industry Exposure

Another important part of our activities was organizing **technical site visits** that allowed students to experience civil engineering beyond the classroom.

We arranged visits to different types of engineering and industrial environments, including **factories, building construction sites, and bridge construction projects**.

These activities required coordination with students, faculty members, companies, and site representatives, as well as managing communication and logistics before and during each visit.

## Working Across Roles

One of the most valuable aspects of my experience was the opportunity to work across **different roles within the association**.

Board members were not restricted to a single function. Depending on the project, we contributed to areas such as:

- Event planning and coordination
- Graphic and visual design
- Social media and communications
- Content creation
- Academic and industry coordination
- Logistics and on-site management
- Team organization

I experienced this rotation of responsibilities across different projects, which gave me a broader understanding of how an academic student organization operates from **initial planning to final execution**.

## Chamroosh

One of my main responsibilities within the association was developing **Chamroosh**, the association's publication.

Together with my friend Mehdi, I helped develop Chamroosh as more than a conventional student magazine. We envisioned it as a platform where civil engineering could be discussed through different perspectives, connecting **technical knowledge with education, industry, technology, culture, and society**.

My work on Chamroosh involved editorial planning, developing content ideas, coordinating contributors, and working across the publication process.

The project became one of my main individual contributions to the association and allowed me to combine **editorial work, communication, design, and project coordination** within an academic environment.

## Recognition

The Civil Engineering Scientific Student Association was recognized in the **Scientific Event** category at *Harekat*, the International Festival of Works and Achievements of Student Scientific Unions and Associations.

This recognition reflected the collective work of the association and the range of scientific, academic, and student activities developed by the team.

## Reflection

My experience on the association's board taught me how to work across different functions while managing projects with a diverse student team.

More importantly, it showed me how a scientific student association can act as a platform connecting **academic knowledge, industry, technology, and student communities**.

The experience strengthened my skills in **teamwork, project coordination, event management, communication, content development, and leadership**, while giving me a broader perspective on the role of civil engineering education beyond the classroom.
