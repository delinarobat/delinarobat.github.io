## TEDxKish 2026 Co-organizer & Curator

**TEDxKish · Kish Island, Iran**  
**Co-organizer & Curator · 2026**  
**Event: 5 November 2026**

I served as a **co-organizer and curator of TEDxKish 2026**, working alongside the main organizer and the wider organizing team. This was my second year on the team, and this time I curated three of the talks myself.

The experience involved **speaker curation, promotion, community outreach, charity work, and event-day coordination**, all under the pressure of the war situation.

Because of the war, we **organized the event from Tehran**, which pushed us to rethink how we reached the people of Kish. Instead of relying on the usual channels, we worked through Kish's own media, took part in charity work on the island, and offered discounts to Kish locals.

## Organizing Under Difficult Conditions

The war situation affected travel, communication, and the way we could promote the event. Instead of treating this as only an obstacle, we tried different approaches to promoting TEDxKish and connecting it with the island.

### Promoting Through Kish's Media

We used and promoted **Kish's own media** to reach the people who live there, so the event felt like it belonged to the island and not just to a team in another city.

### Charity Work in Kish

We carried out **charity work in Kish** as part of the event, so that TEDxKish gave something back to the community that hosted it.

### Discounts for Kish Locals

We offered **discounts for Kish locals**, which helped keep the event open to the people of the island.

## Curating the Talks

As a curator, I worked with three speakers to shape ideas into talks that fit the event. Each of the three talks dealt with a different question that mattered to me: what art does for people in hard times, how to tell real science from claims, and what children need to learn as they grow.

## Omid Nemati

**Talk: Music and Art in Critical Times**

We curated a talk about the role of **music and art in critical times**, including social crises and war.

This theme felt especially close to the situation we were organizing in. The question we wanted the talk to explore was what music and art offer people when everything else feels uncertain.

## Alireza Nikoo

**Talk: Misinformation, Especially in Medicine**

Alireza Nikoo also hosted TEDxKish in 2025. This year we curated a talk about **misinformation, especially in the medical industry**.

The talk was built around a key distinction: a **valid scientific experiment is very different from simply stating a fact**. We wanted the audience to leave with a better sense of how to tell real evidence from a confident claim.

## What I Learned

This second year taught me how an event can adapt when conditions change completely.

Organizing from Tehran for an event on Kish Island meant that we had to think about the audience differently. Using local media, doing charity work, and offering local discounts showed me that **promotion is about building a relationship with a community**, not only announcing an event.

Curating three very different talks, on art in crisis, on misinformation, and on how children grow, also taught me how to help a speaker turn an idea into a talk with a clear purpose.
