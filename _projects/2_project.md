## Media Manager & Curating Team Member

*October 2024*

In October 2024, I joined the TEDxUT team as a **Media Manager and member of the Curating Team**. The experience brought together two areas I particularly enjoy: creative production and collaborative problem-solving.

As part of the media team, I was responsible for coordinating the production and delivery of visual content for the event, while also helping shape how TEDxUT communicated with its audience. At the same time, as a member of the Curating Team, I worked directly with speakers to develop their ideas and transform them into engaging, coherent TED-style talks.

## Media Management

As a Media Manager, I worked on both the creative and organizational sides of the event. I helped coordinate the media team, plan tasks, and keep the production process organized leading up to the event.

We divided responsibilities and tracked our progress using **Google Sheets**, which helped us coordinate deadlines, assign tasks, and make sure different parts of the media production were completed on time.

I also worked on the event's **social media content and visual communication**, helping prepare and organize materials needed before and during the event.

### Responsibilities

- Team coordination and task planning
- Social media management
- Visual content production
- Video editing
- Event communication
- Coordination of media deliverables
- Managing production deadlines

## Design & Creative Production

I used **Figma** to design and organize a range of visual materials for the event, from digital communication pieces to physical event materials. I also worked on video editing and helped prepare content for the event's social media channels.

Beyond social media, the media team was responsible for producing and coordinating a wide range of event materials, including **certificates, presentations, cards, banners, and other visual assets**.

## Curating: “Civilized City”

Alongside my role in media management, I was also part of the **Curating Team**. The theme of our TEDx event was **“Civilized City”**, and our team worked with eight speakers to develop talks that connected their individual experiences and ideas to this broader theme.

I was directly involved in the curating process for **three of the speakers**. My role was to help identify the central idea behind each talk, develop the narrative, and shape their stories into engaging TED-style talks.

The process involved listening carefully to each speaker's ideas, identifying the most meaningful stories and experiences, and helping structure them into a clear narrative. We also considered how each talk could connect to the theme while remaining authentic to the speaker's own perspective.

## What I Took From the Experience

TEDxUT was one of those experiences where the final event was only a small part of the work. Behind the stage was a long process of planning, collaboration, editing, designing, listening, and problem-solving.

Working simultaneously in media management and speaker curation allowed me to experience both sides of communication: **how an idea is developed, and how that idea is eventually presented to an audience.**

It strengthened my ability to work within a team, manage multiple tasks and deadlines, and communicate with people from very different professional backgrounds.

Most importantly, it showed me how much impact thoughtful storytelling can have when creative work, organization, and people come together around a shared idea.
