## BIM Course Project: 4-Storey Residential Building

**University BIM Course**  
**Early 2025**  
**Tools: Revit · Navisworks · Microsoft Project**

In this university BIM course, we took our own apartment building as the case study and simplified it into a **4-storey residential building**. The original building has two units on each floor, and we reduced the project to half of that.

Starting from this building, we went through a complete BIM workflow: describing the structure, planning the construction, modeling in Revit, linking the model to a schedule in Navisworks, and extracting quantities from the model.

## The Workflow

### 1. Describing the Model

We first described the building and gathered information about its **structure and sub-structure**. This gave us the technical basis for everything that followed.

### 2. Construction Task List

We then wrote a **task list for the construction of the project**, breaking the building work down into the activities needed to build it.

### 3. Modeling in Revit

Next, we modeled the building in **Revit**.

### 4. 4D Simulation in Navisworks

We built the construction schedule in **Microsoft Project** and attached it to the Revit model in **Navisworks**. This linked each activity to the parts of the model it builds, so the construction sequence could be visualized over time.

### 5. Quantity Take-off

Finally, we did a **quantity take-off** directly from the Revit model, extracting the quantities of the main building elements. Because the quantities came from the model itself, they updated with any design change instead of being counted by hand from drawings.
